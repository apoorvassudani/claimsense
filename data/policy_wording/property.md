# Northwind Home Shield Policy (fictional) - Policy Wording

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - NOT A REAL POLICY.

## PRP-1 | Documents required for a property claim
A home damage claim must be supported by: (a) the completed claim form, and (b) the contractor's invoice or
repair estimate with itemised materials and labour. Photographs of the damage should be provided where
available.

## PRP-2 | Losses shortly after inception
For loss or damage reported within 30 days of the policy start date, the Company may ask for evidence that the
damage did not exist before the policy began, such as photographs, a surveyor's report or maintenance records.

## PRP-3 | Exclusions
The Company is not liable for gradual deterioration, wear and tear, damp or seepage developing over time, damage
caused by poor workmanship, or loss while the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days.
