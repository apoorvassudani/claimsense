# Northwind Private Car Package Policy (fictional) - Policy Wording

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - NOT A REAL POLICY.

## MTR-1 | Documents required for a motor claim
A motor own-damage claim must be supported by: (a) the completed and signed claim form, (b) the final repair
invoice from the garage with itemised parts and labour, and (c) a copy of the police report, FIR or general
diary entry for the accident, theft or damage. The Company may withhold settlement until all required
documents are received.

## MTR-2 | Insured vehicle
Cover applies only to the vehicle whose registration number is shown in the policy schedule. Damage to any
other vehicle is not covered under own-damage. If the same vehicle is the subject of claims by different
persons, the Company will verify ownership and the registration certificate before settlement.

## MTR-3 | Claims shortly after inception
For a claim arising within 30 days of the policy start date, the Company may require a pre-inspection report,
photographs of the vehicle at inception, or other proof that the damage did not exist before the policy began.

## MTR-4 | Exclusions
The Company is not liable for normal wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, damage while driving
under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or damage while the vehicle is driven by a person without a valid
driving licence.
