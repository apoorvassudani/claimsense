# Northwind Individual Health Policy (fictional) - Policy Wording

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - NOT A REAL POLICY.

## HLT-1 | Documents required for a health claim
A hospitalisation claim must be supported by: (a) the completed claim form, and (b) the final hospital bill with
itemised charges, together with the discharge summary. Claims without the final hospital bill cannot be
assessed.

## HLT-2 | Initial waiting period
No claim is payable for any illness first diagnosed or any hospitalisation that begins within the first 30
days from the policy start date, except hospitalisation caused by an accident. Claims arising in this period
will be checked against medical records to confirm the date the illness started.

## HLT-3 | Hospitalisation dates
The date of loss for a health claim is the date of admission to hospital. Admission and discharge dates shown
on the hospital bill must match those declared in the claim form.

## HLT-4 | Exclusions
The Company is not liable for treatment of pre-existing diseases during the first 36 months of continuous cover,
cosmetic or aesthetic treatment, or expenses that are not medically necessary.
