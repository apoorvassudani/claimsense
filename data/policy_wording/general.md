# Northwind General Insurance Co. Ltd. (fictional) - General Conditions

Applies to every Northwind policy: Private Car Package, Individual Health and Home Shield.
SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - NOT A REAL POLICY.

## GEN-1 | Period of insurance
Cover applies only to loss, damage, illness or injury that occurs during the period of insurance shown in the
policy schedule, between the policy start date and the policy end date, both days inclusive. An event that
happens before the policy starts or after it ends is not covered, even if the claim is reported while the
policy is in force.

## GEN-2 | Claim intimation time limit
The insured must notify the Company of any event that may give rise to a claim as soon as possible and in any
case within 30 days of the date of loss. Claims intimated after 30 days may be considered only if the insured
gives a satisfactory written reason for the delay. Late reporting alone does not void a claim, but the Company
may verify the circumstances in more detail.

## GEN-3 | Sum insured is the maximum liability
The sum insured shown in the schedule is the maximum amount the Company will pay for all claims during the
period of insurance. Any amount claimed above the remaining sum insured is not payable. A claim that exceeds
the sum insured will be assessed only up to the available limit.

## GEN-4 | Claim amount must be supported by documents
The Company pays the assessed amount of the loss, supported by original bills, invoices and receipts. Where the
amount stated in the claim form differs from the amount shown on the supporting bills or invoices, the
Company will rely on the verified documents and may ask the insured to explain the difference.

## GEN-5 | Accuracy of information and misrepresentation
All information in the claim form and supporting documents must be true and consistent. The name of the
insured, the policy number and the dates of loss shown on every document must match the policy schedule and
the claim form. Inconsistent names, policy numbers or dates must be explained and may delay the claim.
Deliberate misstatement of any material fact entitles the Company to reject the claim.

## GEN-6 | Fraud
If any claim is fraudulent in any respect, including the submission of forged, altered or reused documents, a
bill or invoice already presented in support of another claim, or the same document submitted for more than
one claim, all benefits under the claim are forfeited and the Company may cancel the policy and recover any
amount already paid.

## GEN-7 | Right to investigate linked claims
The Company may investigate any claim before settlement. Claims that share contact details such as a phone
number with other claimants, involve a vehicle claimed by different people, or cluster around the same
hospital, garage or contractor within a short period may be referred to the special investigation unit for
verification. Referral is a review step, not a finding of fraud.
